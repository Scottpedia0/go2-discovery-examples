Appendix: Form & Data Quality Issues

Technical details on form field problems. These are platform fixes, not people problems.

feeling_selected Field -- Dead Data

81.6% stuck at 2▶

Across 1,272 EOD reports over 6 months, 1,038 (81.6%) report feeling_selected = 2. Seven EMs report 2 on 90%+ of days. Harlon and Pao report 2 on 100% of days -- not once in 89 and 112 reports respectively did they choose a different number.

Meanwhile, 104 EODs with feeling=2 contain distress keywords ("frustrated," "failed," "messed up," "ghosting") in their free-text fields. The real emotional data lives in the headlines and news_worthy fields -- not the structured feeling field.

Fix: Remove the default value. Force a conscious selection before the form submits. One platform change.

need_help Field -- Culturally Non-Functional

98.5% always "no"▶

Only 19 total instances of need_help = "yes" across the entire team over 6 months. Five EMs never once said yes. Harlon clicked yes once but wrote "na" in the details field.

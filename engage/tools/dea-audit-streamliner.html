    Calibration Tips

    Common Scoring Mistakes

    • Leniency bias: Scoring 4 when the call is actually a 3. Ask: "Would I use this as a training example?"
    • Halo effect: One strong moment inflates the whole score. Score the full call, not just highlights.
    • Recency bias: Strong close masks weak opening. Weight all segments equally.
    • Central tendency: Defaulting to 3 on everything. Use the full scale.
    • Zooming out too much: Missing specific behaviors. Anchor observations to timestamps.

    The "Zoom Out" Technique

    After noting specific moments, step back and ask: What was the OVERALL coaching quality? Did the BW leave better equipped than when they started? Would a new EM learn good habits from watching this call? The answers to these questions should align with your score.

    Pre-Calibration Checklist

    • Review your 3 most borderline scores
    • Write a 1-sentence justification for each score
    • Identify the 1 call you're least confident about
    • Note any calls where you changed your score after first watch
    • Compare your average score to the team's historical average (3.1-3.4)
